# Ultraglass Code of Conduct

Ultraglass is a technical project and its community includes maintainers, contributors, network operators, and people learning how network services work. Everyone should be able to participate without harassment or personal attacks.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful when discussing code, infrastructure, documentation, and people.
- Assume good intent, ask for clarification, and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Keep feedback specific and useful. Critique the change rather than the person.
- Protect private information and do not publish credentials, personal data, or private network details.
- Follow the project licence and respect the work of upstream contributors.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualised conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting a problem

Open an issue using the project's reporting tools, or contact a maintainer privately through the contact details published on the repository profile. Do not include sensitive information in a public issue. Reports will be handled as discreetly as the circumstances allow.

This code applies to the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, documentation, and project discussions. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to keep these spaces safe and useful.
